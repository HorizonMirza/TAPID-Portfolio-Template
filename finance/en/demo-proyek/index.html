Projects

  1. Budget analysis

    Budget Bridge

    A simulated budget report with a waterfall chart that explains the move from a budget of IDR 500 million to actual spending of IDR 530 million through three cost drivers.

  2. Cash flow forecasting

    Cash Flow Map

    A five-week cash forecast that shows closing balances and the lowest cash point. Assumption notes tie balance changes to supplier payments and customer receipts.

  3. Management reporting

    Monthly Close Pack

    A month-end close pack summarizing cash, receivables, inventory, and expense reconciliation. Row-level notes show what needs to be resolved before reporting.

  4. Receivables analysis

    Invoice Aging

    An aging dashboard that groups invoices by how far they are overdue. Its totals and priority list help teams decide which receivables to follow up first.

  5. Profitability analysis

    Margin Lens

    A gross-margin comparison model for three fictional product categories. The table shows revenue, costs, and margins so changes can be traced to each category.

  6. Scenario planning

    Forecast Skenario

    A forecast model comparing conservative, base, and optimistic cases with different growth assumptions. The table shows their effects on revenue and gross profit.
