Projects

  1. Poster study

    Lipat / Lepas

    A poster study that uses a folded paper sculpture as the focal point. Side lighting casts a diagonal shadow across the red field to explore eye movement and the balance of negative space.

  2. Visual identity

    Cetak Pasar

    A visual identity for a fictional independent print fair. Red, cream, and black contrast and a circular stamp motif carry across the main poster, admission ticket, and participant card.

  3. Editorial design

    Kota Setelah Hujan

    A cover and opening spread for a visual essay about the city after rain. A serif title, street illustration, and short columns set the pace between imagery and narrative.

  4. Music event poster

    Nada Jalanan

    A poster for a fictional music event. Concentric lines evoke vinyl records, and tight typography gives the main poster and programme a strong contrast.

  5. Packaging identity

    Ramu Rasa

    A packaging study for a fictional spice drink. The bold wordmark, striped field, and warm palette carry across the front label and ingredient card.

  6. Wayfinding system

    Arah Festival

    A wayfinding system for a fictional festival. Consistent colour, label size, and arrow direction distinguish the stage, workshop, and entrance.
