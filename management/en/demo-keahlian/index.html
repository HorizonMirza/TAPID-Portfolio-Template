Projects

  1. Project delivery

    Rencana Buka Cabang

    A six-week operating plan for a simulated branch opening. The schedule sequences permits, procurement, setup, training, and final testing so dependencies are visible.

  2. Team planning

    Tinjauan Kapasitas

    A two-week capacity review comparing four team functions. The chart shows where teams are nearing capacity, while the table records decisions to defer work, accept it, or reserve time.

  3. Process design

    Serah Terima Layanan

    A three-stage handover board for moving a service into operations. Cards show who owns access, documentation, escalation, and support contacts, alongside each item’s status.

  4. Priority management

    Papan Prioritas Sprint

    A weekly priority board combining requests, owners, and decisions. The team can see which work should start, wait, or move into review.

  5. Process mapping

    Peta Alur Pesanan

    A process map tracing orders from intake through shipping confirmation. Each stage records its owner, output, and what should trigger another check.

  6. Team onboarding

    Rencana Orientasi Tim

    A five-day onboarding plan covering workspace access, observation, and a practice task. Completion criteria help mentors assess readiness.
