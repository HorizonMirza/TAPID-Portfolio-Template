Projects

  1. Community research

    Cerita dari Sudut Kota

    A fieldnote synthesis for observing shared spaces. Simulated notes are grouped into access, usage times, and maintenance, then turned into an agenda for the next visit.

  2. Writing

    Catatan untuk Relawan

    A one-page guide for reading room volunteers. Two checklists separate opening preparations from closing tasks so the next shift can pick up where the previous one left off.

  3. Program coordination

    Agenda Ruang Baca

    A community calendar for a reading session, book swap, and zine workshop. Each entry gives the date, time, and what participants should bring or prepare.

  4. Interview guide

    Wawancara Warga

    A semi-structured interview guide for understanding how community spaces are used. Prompts move from daily experience to barriers and suggestions.

  5. Accessibility research

    Peta Akses Komunitas

    An observation sheet for signage, entry paths, seating, and information access in a simulated community space. The notes help prioritise simple fixes.

  6. Community publication

    Buletin Tetangga

    A one-page neighbourhood newsletter concept. The sample issue groups content into a lead story, the coming week’s agenda, and an invitation to contribute.
